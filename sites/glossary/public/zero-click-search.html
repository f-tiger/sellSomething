What is Zero-click search?

Definition: A zero-click search is a search where the user gets their answer directly on the results page — from an AI Overview, featured snippet or knowledge panel — without clicking through to any website.

Zero-click searches climbed past 69% of queries in 2025 and are projected higher as AI answers expand. It means less referral traffic from informational queries, and makes being the cited source (rather than the clicked link) the thing worth optimizing for.

Is SEO dead because of zero-click search?

No, but its goal changes. Informational-query traffic shrinks; the win shifts to being cited in AI answers (GEO) and capturing transactional and brand demand.
