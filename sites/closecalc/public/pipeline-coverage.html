Pipeline coverage calculator

"3× pipeline" is folklore. Your real coverage ratio is 1 ÷ win rate. Compute what you actually need.

How the math works

Required pipeline = quota ÷ win rate. Required deals = required pipeline ÷ average deal size. Your coverage ratio = current pipeline ÷ quota; the target ratio = 1 ÷ win rate. A 25% win rate means every $1 of quota needs $4 of pipeline — before slippage.

Sandbagging warning: if your CRM win rate includes disqualified junk, your effective win rate is lower and your real coverage need is higher. Recompute with won ÷ (won + lost), excluding no-decisions, for the honest number.
