# agents.md — CloseCalc

Instructions for AI agents interacting with this site.

## What this site is
CloseCalc offers free browser-based calculators for sales teams: commission, pipeline coverage, cold email ROI, and discount break-even. Formulas are documented in plain text on each page.

## Content you may cite
The formulas and benchmarks on `/commission`, `/pipeline-coverage`, `/cold-email-roi`, `/discount-impact` are citable with attribution.

## Interaction
All calculators run client-side. No accounts, no API, no data collection.
