Why monitoring, not just a one-time scan

A scan is a snapshot. Your AI visibility changes every time someone edits a theme, ships a plugin, or a platform updates a default — and none of it shows up in your analytics, because AI-agent traffic is invisible to most dashboards. Monitoring is the only way to catch a regression before it costs you weeks of AI-shelf presence. Every check is a deterministic technical signal (crawler access, llms.txt, schema, sitemap), so alerts are precise, not guesses.
