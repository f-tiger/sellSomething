Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-21 · AgentReady, agentready.agiscorecard.com ("we", "us")

What we collect

Email addresses you submit (waitlists, monitoring, purchases), the URLs you ask us to scan, and standard technical logs. Payments are handled by our merchant of record (Paddle/Creem) — we never see card numbers.

How we use it

To deliver the service you asked for: run scans, send monitoring alerts and purchase deliverables (license keys, audit tokens), and account administration. We use Google Analytics for aggregate site usage.

Storage & sharing

Data is stored in Cloudflare Workers KV. We do not sell personal data. Sub-processors: Cloudflare (hosting), Resend (transactional email), Paddle/Creem (payments), Google (analytics).

Your rights

Email us to access or delete your data at any time; monitoring can be stopped by replying to any alert email.
