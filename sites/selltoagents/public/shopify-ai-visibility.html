Is your Shopify store visible to ChatGPT?

Direct answer: If you're on Shopify you're already enrolled in ChatGPT Shopping and Google AI Mode — but enrollment ≠ visibility. Shopify handles llms.txt and agents.md natively since May 2026. What still decides whether agents pick you: robots.txt hygiene, Product/Offer schema completeness, feed quality (GTINs, availability), and FAQ content. Verify in 10 seconds with a free scan.

What Shopify does for you (don't rebuild it)

  • /llms.txt and /agents.md — served natively, customizable via theme templates.
  • ACP enrollment — ChatGPT Shopping product feeds flow automatically for eligible merchants.
  • Base Product JSON-LD — most modern themes emit it on product pages.

What's still on you

  1. robots.txt hygiene. Apps and custom robots.txt.liquid edits can block GPTBot/ClaudeBot/PerplexityBot without you noticing. This is the #1 silent killer we see.
  2. Schema completeness. Theme JSON-LD often misses GTIN, shippingDetails and MerchantReturnPolicy — the fields agents filter on ("free returns", "arrives by Friday").
  3. Feed hygiene. Missing GTINs, stale availability and truncated titles degrade selection in every AI channel at once.
  4. FAQ content. FAQPage schema is among the most-cited structures in AI answers; most Shopify themes don't ship it.

15-minute Shopify verification routine

  1. Open yourstore.com/robots.txt — confirm no AI crawler is disallowed.
  2. Open yourstore.com/llms.txt — confirm it renders and the facts are current.
  3. Run a free AgentReady scan — it checks all 7 crawlers, schema, llms.txt, agents.md and sitemap in one pass.
  4. Ask ChatGPT to recommend products in your category — note whether you appear. That's your citation share.
