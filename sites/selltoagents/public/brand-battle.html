FAQ

Where do the scores come from?

Every score comes from the DTC AI Visibility Index: each brand's homepage is scanned live by the free AgentReady scanner across 19 machine-readable signals AI shopping agents rely on — crawler access, structured data, llms.txt, sitemap and more — scored 0–100 and updated weekly.

What does winning actually mean?

The winner is simply the brand with the higher AI-visibility score — its store is more machine-readable to AI shopping agents like ChatGPT, Claude and Perplexity. A high score doesn't guarantee recommendations, but a low one nearly guarantees invisibility.

Can I battle my own store?

The roster is limited to the brands in the index, but you can get the exact same scan for your own store — free, in about 10 seconds — with AgentReady.
