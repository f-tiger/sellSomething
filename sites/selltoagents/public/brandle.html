FAQ

How is the daily brand chosen?

By hashing the current UTC date over the DTC AI Visibility Index roster — deterministic, so every player worldwide gets the same puzzle. A fresh one drops at midnight UTC, and your streak counts consecutive daily wins.

Where do the clues come from?

Every clue is real data: each brand's homepage is scanned by the free AgentReady scanner across 19 machine-readable signals AI shopping agents rely on — that scan produces the category, grade, failing checks, score and rank the game reveals.

Can I play more than once a day?

The daily puzzle is one per UTC day, but after finishing it you can play unlimited practice rounds with random brands. Practice never touches your streak.
