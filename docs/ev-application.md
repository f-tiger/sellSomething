**Project title**: AgentFront — agent-commerce onboarding for long-tail merchants

**Amount requested**: $15,000

**Summary (≤200 words)**

AI shopping agents (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude) are becoming a real acquisition channel, but the tooling assumes an enterprise: Cloudflare and HubSpot grade your "agent readiness" for free, while nobody helps a small merchant actually *become* agent-ready. I am building the missing layer for long-tail SMBs: automated generation and continuous verification of the machine-readable surface a store needs (llms.txt, agents.md, Product/Offer structured data, ACP/UCP feeds), delivered as self-serve tools rather than consulting.

Shipped so far, solo, with heavy AI automation: a free agent-readiness scanner with continuous monitoring and alerting (agentready.agiscorecard.com), an MCP server health checker with a weekly public index (mcppulse.agiscorecard.com), a merchant education site, calculators, and open developer tooling (editor plugins for llms.txt/agents.md, pay-per-result audit APIs that AI agents can invoke and pay for autonomously via x402). Everything deploys automatically from one repository; the marginal cost of an additional check, page, or report is near zero.

The grant funds 12 months of infrastructure and distribution experiments to find repeatable demand among US/EU SMB merchants before agent-commerce standards consolidate.
